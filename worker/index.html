Cedwyn Estate in 3D

The historic parcel layer follows the Stewart plan of Cedfin alias Tŷ Mawr as traced through the 1841 Llandough, Cogan and Leckwith tithe map and aligned to modern mapping. Toggle Parcels to explore the 59-number register; tap an outline or number to inspect its transcribed tithe details and source status. Grouped and unplaced entries remain identified rather than being given invented boundaries.

The surrounding ground surface and buildings are still schematic in this version. The parcel overlay uses the georeferenced H3 source geometry; it is not yet a LiDAR-derived elevation surface or photogrammetric reconstruction. Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom, and tap a building to open its site.

Tap Roadmap for the disclosure trails: one path per institution from Tŷ Mawr, climbing from the first request through the deadline, internal review, the ICO, the Tribunal, complaint and the pre-action letter to court. Branches show where a reply ends a trail or opens the trigger event procedure; red crosses mark routes that close. The walker on each trail stands at today's date.
